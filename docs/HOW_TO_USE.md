# Symbern Fleet Intelligence — How to Use

A quick guide to finding your way around. Nothing to install — it runs in your
browser.

## Signing in
- Open the link you were sent and enter your email and password.
- First load after the app has been idle can take ~30–60 seconds to wake up, then
  it's quick.

## The two views
Use the **Fleet / Production** toggle at the top of the page to switch:
- **Fleet** — battery packs deployed in the field (live health monitoring)
- **Production** — manufacturing / QA test records

---

## Fleet view — monitoring deployed batteries
- **The grid:** every card is a battery pack, sorted worst-first, showing state of
  charge, pack voltage, current, temperature, and any active alarms.
- **Filter quickly:** click the **Total / Critical / Warning / OK** tallies, type in
  the **search** box, or open **⚙ Filters** for site, company, equipment, a state-
  of-charge range, or "only packs with active alarms."
- **✨ Ask AI:** type a request in plain English (e.g. *"warning packs below 25%"*)
  and it filters the fleet for you.
- **AI Briefing:** get a short written summary of overall fleet health.
- **Active Alerts:** the panel lists current alerts newest-first — click one to jump
  to that pack.
- **Open a pack:** click any card to see live **state-of-charge and voltage trend
  charts**, its details, and **notes** you can add.
- **Reports & export:** **📄 Daily Report** (with **Print / Save-as-PDF**), and
  **⬇ CSV / Excel** exports that match your current filters.
- **↻ Refresh** updates on demand; the **⚡ Realtime** badge means it's updating live.

## Production view — manufacturing / QA
- **Daily Production Summary:** total tested, passed, failed, pass %/fail %, and the
  most-failed product, fixture, and station. Use the **date picker** to change the day.
- **Pass/Fail lookup:** the table lists test records; filter by product, station,
  fixture, test parameter, result, serial, or date range.
- **Universal search:** one bar searches across serial number, part number, product,
  station, fixture, and operator.
- **Serial history:** click any **serial number** to see that unit's full test
  history — every test, station, result, and any failure reason.
- **Export:** **⬇ CSV / Excel** for the current view.

---

## Getting help or leaving feedback
Click **💬 Assistant** (bottom-right) any time. You can:
- Ask how something works ("How do I export failed tests?")
- Click **🎫 Start a support ticket** to send a question or request to IT or management.

## Good to know
- What you see is **simulated data** for this preview — the next step is connecting
  your real equipment and systems.
- Your access depends on your **role**: viewers can look but not edit; engineers and
  supervisors can add notes; administrators can also manage users.
